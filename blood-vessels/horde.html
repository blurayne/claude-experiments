How it works

Blood flows from the arterial trunk through the branches and the narrow AV connectors into the veins. Your neutrophils swim, but the current decides how fast: downstream is quick, upstream in a fast artery is near-impossible — go round through the veins and the heart instead.

Infection sites bloom in the vessel walls and shed viruses and bacteria into the flow. Touch a pathogen to engulf it, crowd a lesion to destroy it. Pathogens that escape into the veins and every living one raise the infection level; at 100 % the tissue is lost. Destroy the lesions of all 8 waves to clear the infection. Idle cells stick to the wall so the flow does not wash them away.

Settings → Vessel look → Glass turns the vessels into see-through tubes, so you can watch your horde inside arteries and veins at every zoom.
