An interactive microbe atlas.

An atlas of the very small — the cells you are built from and the parts inside them, the genetic material they carry, the microbes that help and harm us and our pets, and a few things people have engineered. Every subject is drawn five ways: a real micrograph wherever a freely licensed one exists, plus four generated from a researched reference and checked back against it. Switch the wording between kids, adults and scientists, switch language, and choose which pictures sit side by side. Styles marked “·gen” are generated; where a “Real” picture is a structural model rather than a photograph, or shows a close relative because no image of the species is free to use, its caption says so.
