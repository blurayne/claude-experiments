## Cancer cell (malignant neoplasm) (`cancer-cell`)
A cell of the body that has lost control of its own division through accumulated mutations. The classic cytological signs are an enlarged, irregular nucleus with prominent nucleoli, a raised nucleus-to-cytoplasm ratio, an irregular cell outline and atypical mitotic figures — while ordinary organelles (mitochondria, ER, ribosomes) are still present, because it is still a human cell.
