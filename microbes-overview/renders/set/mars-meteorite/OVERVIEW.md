## ALH 84001 (Mars meteorite) (`alh-84001`)
Not an organism: a 1931 g lump of Martian orthopyroxenite found in the Allan Hills, Antarctica, on 27 December 1984. In 1996 a NASA team led by David McKay reported four findings in it that together looked like traces of past life; every one now has a non-biological explanation. The atlas closes here because the testing is the lesson, not the claim.
