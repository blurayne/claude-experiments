## 1. Scientific reference (the yardstick for verification)

Human rhinovirus is one of the **smallest and structurally simplest** human viruses: a **naked (non-enveloped) icosahedral particle only ~30 nm across** — roughly a quarter the diameter of an influenza virion and about a third of SARS-CoV-2. There is **no lipid envelope and no surface spikes at all**; the outer boundary is a bare protein shell. That protein shell (**capsid**) is built from **60 identical protomers** arranged with **pseudo-T=3 (P=3) icosahedral symmetry**, each protomer made of four polypeptides: **VP1, VP2 and VP3** form the outer surface, while **VP4** is small, myristoylated and lies entirely on the **inner** face of the shell against the RNA. VP1 clusters around each of the twelve **five-fold axes** to form a raised **star-shaped mesa (plateau)**, VP2/VP3 alternate around the three-fold axes to make a shallow **"propeller"** relief, and between mesa and propeller runs a continuous **circular depression around each five-fold vertex — the "canyon"**.

The canyon is the functional heart of the particle. In **major-group** rhinoviruses (~90 % of A and B types) it is the binding site for the host receptor **ICAM-1**; minor-group types instead use **LDL-receptor** family proteins on the outer surface, and species **RV-C** has a shallow, collapsed canyon and binds **CDHR3**. Immediately **beneath the canyon floor**, inside VP1, sits a **hydrophobic pocket** occupied by a fatty-acid–like lipid, the **"pocket factor"**, which stabilises the capsid; capsid-binder antivirals (pleconaril and relatives) displace it and lock the particle so it cannot uncoat.

Inside the shell there is **no nucleocapsid protein, no matrix layer and no packaged polymerase**. The genome is a **single, continuous, positive-sense single-stranded RNA molecule of ~7.2 kb**, loosely coiled in the capsid interior, carrying the small viral protein **VPg (3B)** covalently attached to its **5' end** instead of a cap, a long structured 5'-UTR containing an **IRES**, one **single open reading frame** encoding a polyprotein, and a **3' poly(A) tail**. Because it is a plus-strand RNA, it is directly readable by host ribosomes — the virus does not need to bring a polymerase along.

Biology worth carrying into the pictures and text: rhinoviruses replicate optimally at **33-35 °C**, which is why they thrive in the **cooler nasal passages and upper airway** rather than in the warm lower lung; they are **acid-labile** (unlike enteroviruses proper, they are destroyed by stomach acid); they spread by **hand contact with contaminated surfaces and by droplets**, survive hours on doorknobs and toys, and being non-enveloped they are **resistant to alcohol gels and detergents** — which is exactly why **soap-and-water hand washing** is the effective defence. There are **~170 genotypes**, so immunity to one gives little protection against the next, and no vaccine exists; a cold is cleared by the body itself within about a week.

Sources: [ViralZone / Expasy — Rhinovirus A](https://viralzone.expasy.org/659), [ICTV Report — *Picornaviridae*](https://ictv.global/report/chapter/picornaviridae/picornaviridae), [NCBI Bookshelf — Baron's *Medical Microbiology*, ch. 53 "Rhinoviruses"](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK7967/), [Rossmann et al., "Structure of a human common cold virus…", *Nature* 317:145 (1985) — the canyon hypothesis](https://www.nature.com/articles/317145a0), [PDB 4RHV / 1AYM entries for HRV14 and HRV16](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4RHV), [CDC — Rhinoviruses / Common Cold](https://www.cdc.gov/common-cold/about/index.html).

### Do NOT draw (scientifically misleading)

- **A lipid envelope / membrane bilayer of any kind** — rhinovirus is **naked**. A soap-bubble membrane around the capsid is the single most common error.
- **Surface spikes, a corona of clubs, HA/NA-style projections or fuzzy fringe** — there are **no glycoprotein spikes**; the relief is only the mesa / canyon / propeller sculpting of the shell itself.
- **A perfectly smooth golf-ball or a plain geometric d20** — the shell must show the three-fold propeller facets, the raised five-fold star mesas and the canyon groove around them.
- **DNA, a double helix, or base-pair rungs** — the genome is **single-stranded RNA**.
- **A segmented genome / several separate RNA pieces** — unlike influenza, there is exactly **ONE continuous RNA molecule**.
- **A packaged RNA polymerase, a matrix layer, a helical nucleocapsid, or nucleoprotein beads coating the RNA** — the interior holds only the bare RNA (plus VPg) against VP4.
- **A nucleus, ribosomes, mitochondria, cytoplasm or any cell organelle** — a virion is not a cell.
- **A bacteriophage tail, base plate, legs, or head-and-tail body.**
- **Anything at influenza/SARS-CoV-2 scale** — if shown beside other viruses it must read as clearly the smallest.
- **A face, eyes or anthropomorphism** (except in the separate kids' coloring page), and **no text, letters, numbers, scale bars or watermarks**.

## 2. Real microscopy reference (own set `reference-microscopy`)

**Provenance caveat, stated plainly:** there is **no properly-licensed conventional TEM or SEM plate of a rhinovirus virion** to be had. CDC PHIL has none (its picornavirus TEMs are poliovirus and enterovirus 70/coxsackievirus A24v), the NIAID Flickr stream has none, and Wikimedia Commons carries only **X-ray-derived surface renderings** (`Rhinovirus isosurface.png`, `Rhinovirus.PNG`) plus journal figure panels — no micrograph. **Adults (popular science, health).**  
🇬🇧 Rhinoviruses cause the majority of common colds — an estimated half to three quarters of all upper-respiratory infections — and are the reason adults average two to three colds a year. They belong to the Picornaviridae and are structurally minimal: a naked protein shell about 30 nm wide, with no envelope and no surface spikes, wrapped around a single strand of positive-sense RNA. Two practical consequences follow from that shell. First, rhinoviruses replicate best at 33-35 °C, the temperature of the nasal passages, which keeps most infections in the upper airway; the symptoms you feel — congestion, runny nose, sore throat — are largely your own inflammatory response, not damage done by the virus. Second, having no lipid envelope makes them tough: they survive for hours on hands, doorknobs and toys and shrug off alcohol hand gels far better than flu or coronaviruses do, so soap-and-water hand washing and not touching your face are the interventions that actually work. With around 170 genotypes, immunity to one does little against the next, no vaccine exists, and no antiviral is licensed; the illness resolves on its own in roughly a week. For people with asthma or COPD, though, a rhinovirus infection is a leading trigger of exacerbations and deserves an action plan rather than a shrug.

**Scientific.**  
🇬🇧 Human rhinoviruses (family Picornaviridae, genus Enterovirus; species Rhinovirus A, B and C, ~170 genotypes) are non-enveloped icosahedral virions of ~30 nm. The capsid has pseudo-T=3 (P=3) symmetry and comprises 60 protomers of VP1, VP2 and VP3, which form the outer surface, plus internal myristoylated VP4. VP1 pentamers create a raised star-shaped mesa at each five-fold axis, VP2/VP3 form the three-fold propeller, and a circular depression — the canyon — encircles each vertex. Major-group A and B serotypes bind ICAM-1 within the canyon, minor-group types bind LDL-receptor family members on the exposed surface, and RV-C, which has a shallow canyon, uses CDHR3. A hydrophobic pocket in VP1 beneath the canyon floor holds a lipid pocket factor that stabilises the capsid and is displaced by capsid-binding antivirals such as pleconaril. The genome is a single ~7.2 kb positive-sense ssRNA with a covalently linked VPg (3B) at the 5' end, a type-I IRES in the 5' UTR, one open reading frame encoding a polyprotein processed by the 2A and 3C proteinases, and a 3' poly(A) tail; no polymerase is packaged. Replication is optimal at 33-35 °C, restricting most infection to the upper airway, and the virion is acid-labile, distinguishing rhinoviruses from other enteroviruses. Clinical illness is driven predominantly by host cytokine and neurogenic responses rather than cytolysis, and rhinovirus is the principal viral trigger of asthma and COPD exacerbations.  
🇩🇪 Humane Rhinoviren (Familie Picornaviridae, Gattung Enterovirus; Spezies Rhinovirus A, B und C, rund 170 Genotypen) sind unbehüllte ikosaedrische Virionen von etwa 30 nm. Das Kapsid besitzt Pseudo-T=3-(P=3-)Symmetrie und besteht aus 60 Protomeren mit VP1, VP2 und VP3 an der Außenfläche sowie dem innen liegenden, myristoylierten VP4. VP1-Pentamere bilden an jeder fünfzähligen Achse ein erhabenes, sternförmiges Plateau, VP2/VP3 formen den dreizähligen Propeller, und eine ringförmige Vertiefung — der Canyon — umläuft jeden Vertex. Serotypen der Major-Gruppe (A und B) binden ICAM-1 im Canyon, Minor-Gruppen-Typen binden Mitglieder der LDL-Rezeptorfamilie an der freiliegenden Oberfläche, und RV-C mit seinem flachen Canyon nutzt CDHR3. Eine hydrophobe Tasche in VP1 unter dem Canyonboden enthält einen lipidischen Pocket-Faktor, der das Kapsid stabilisiert und von kapsidbindenden Virostatika wie Pleconaril verdrängt wird. Das Genom ist eine einzelne, etwa 7,2 kb lange Plusstrang-ssRNA mit kovalent gebundenem VPg (3B) am 5'-Ende, einer IRES vom Typ I in der 5'-UTR, einem einzigen offenen Leserahmen für ein Polyprotein, das von den Proteinasen 2A und 3C prozessiert wird, sowie einem 3'-Poly(A)-Schwanz; eine Polymerase wird nicht mitverpackt. Die Replikation verläuft optimal bei 33-35 °C, was die Infektion überwiegend auf die oberen Atemwege beschränkt, und das Virion ist säurelabil, was Rhinoviren von den übrigen Enteroviren abgrenzt. Die Symptomatik entsteht vorwiegend durch Zytokin- und neurogene Wirtsreaktionen und weniger durch Zytolyse; Rhinoviren sind zudem der wichtigste virale Auslöser von Asthma- und COPD-Exazerbationen.

### Real microscopy reference (`reference-microscopy`)
- `cryo-EM` · CC0 1.0 / public domain (wwPDB EMDB entry image) · EMDB EMD-12171 'HRV14 native particle' — Hrebik D, Fuzik T, Gondova M, Smerdova L, Adamopoulos A, Sedo O, Zdrahal Z, Plevka P (2021) PNAS 118:e2024251118; cryo-EM reconstruction, radial colouring — ✅ PASS (with a provenance caveat) — no properly-licensed conventional TEM/SEM plate of a rhinovirus virion exists in CDC PHIL, the NIAID Flickr stream or Wikimedia Commons (Commons carries only X-ray-derived renderings), so the reference is the wwPDB EMDB entry image for EMD-12171, a genuine cryo-electron-microscopy reconstruction of a native human rhinovirus 14 particle (Hrebik et al. 2021, PNAS), CC0. It shows exactly what the science section describes: a single naked icosahedral capsid with radially false-coloured relief — yellow star-shaped mesas at the five-fold vertices, blue canyon depressions ringing them and green propeller facets between — and no envelope or spikes.
