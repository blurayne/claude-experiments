## 1. Scientific reference (the yardstick for verification)

Norovirus is a **small, naked (non-enveloped) icosahedral RNA virus** of the family **Caliciviridae**, genus *Norovirus*. The virion is **round-to-hexagonal in outline and about 38-40 nm across** (ICTV gives 35-40 nm for caliciviruses generally; the classic negative-stain plates of human norovirus measure 27-38 nm depending on stain penetration) — a particle of roughly **0.5 femtogram**, one of the smallest things in this atlas. There is **no lipid envelope, no membrane and no glycoprotein spikes at all**: the outer boundary is bare protein, and that is the single most important fact for every render.

The capsid is the simplest possible design and the most distinctive-looking. It is built from **180 copies of one single major structural protein, VP1**, arranged with **T = 3 icosahedral symmetry** as **90 dimers**. Each VP1 folds into two parts joined by a short hinge: an N-terminal **shell (S) domain**, an eight-stranded antiparallel β-barrel that tiles into the **contiguous inner shell** enclosing the genome; and a C-terminal **protruding (P) domain** that sticks outward. Two P domains from neighbouring subunits clasp into an **arch-shaped dimer** — the "capsomere" you actually see in profile — whose **P1 subdomain forms the legs** of the arch and whose **hypervariable P2 subdomain forms the outward-facing head**. P2 carries the **histo-blood-group antigen (HBGA) binding site** and the main neutralising epitopes, which is why a person's secretor/blood-group status influences susceptibility and why the virus evolves so fast at that surface.

Because 90 arches rise off an otherwise continuous shell, the space **between** them is sunk into **32 evenly spaced, round, cup-shaped depressions — 12 at the icosahedral five-fold axes and 20 at the three-fold axes**. These calices are what Caliciviridae is named for (Latin *calix*, "cup" or "goblet"), and they are exactly what makes a norovirus particle read as a **knobbly, dimpled ball rather than a smooth sphere** in electron micrographs. They are **hollows**, an optical consequence of the arching P dimers around them — they are **never spikes, rays or stalks**, and drawing them as protrusions inverts the whole morphology.

Inside the shell sits a **single, continuous, positive-sense single-stranded RNA molecule of ~7.5 kb** (ICTV: 7.4-8.3 kb for the family; human norovirus genomes cluster around 7.5-7.7 kb). It carries no cap; instead the small viral protein **VPg is covalently linked to its 5' end**, and the 3' end is **polyadenylated**. Because it is plus-strand RNA, host ribosomes read it directly — the virion carries **no packaged polymerase, no matrix layer and no nucleocapsid protein**. A handful of copies of the **minor structural protein VP2** ride inside, associated with the S-domain face of the shell.

**Contrast with rotavirus**, which is already in this atlas and is the other great cause of viral gastroenteritis: rotavirus is a **triple-layered ~75 nm wheel with 11 segments of double-stranded RNA and a packaged polymerase**; norovirus is a **single-layered ~38 nm shell with one continuous plus-strand RNA**. If both appear side by side they must be unmistakably different objects.

Biology worth carrying into the pictures and the text: norovirus is a **gut virus**, replicating in the **enterocytes of the small intestine** (and, in the murine model, in tuft cells), which is why illness is sudden vomiting plus watery, non-bloody diarrhoea rather than a respiratory syndrome. **Incubation is 6-50 hours and the illness usually lasts 12-48 hours.** Transmission is **faecal-oral** — person to person, contaminated food and water (shellfish, salads), and contaminated surfaces — with billions of particles shed per gram of stool and vomit. The **infectious dose is famously tiny: RKI cites 10-100 virus particles, with as few as 10 sufficient**, which is why outbreaks tear through cruise ships, schools, care homes and hospital wards. Being **non-enveloped it is highly environment-stable and resists alcohol**: CDC states plainly that hand sanitiser does not work well against norovirus and that **hand washing with soap and water for at least 20 seconds** is the effective measure; German practice (RKI) requires disinfectants declared **"begrenzt viruzid PLUS"** or **"viruzid"**, not ordinary limited-virucidal alcohol rubs. There is no licensed vaccine and no specific antiviral; treatment is **rehydration** — small sips, often.

### Do NOT draw (scientifically misleading)

- **ANY lipid envelope, membrane bilayer, soap-bubble halo or greasy coat** — norovirus is **naked**. This is the most common and most serious error, and it also contradicts the teaching point that alcohol gel works poorly against it.
- **Coronavirus-style long stalked club spikes, a corona of knobs, HA/NA-style projections, radiating rays or a fuzzy fringe** — the relief is only the arching P-domain dimers of the shell itself. Never call or draw them spikes.
- **A smooth featureless sphere, a plain golf ball, a billiard ball or a bare geometric d20** — the 90 arches and the 32 cup-shaped hollows must read clearly; a smooth ball is a FAIL.
- **A helical, rod-shaped, filamentous or pleomorphic capsid** — the particle is icosahedral.
- **DNA, a double helix or base-pair rungs** — the genome is **single-stranded RNA**.
- **A segmented genome, several separate RNA pieces, or eleven little rods** — that is rotavirus; norovirus has exactly **ONE continuous RNA molecule**.
- **A triple-layered rotavirus-style shell** (core + VP6 middle layer + VP7 outer layer), **VP4 spikes**, or a **wheel / hub-and-spoke silhouette** — norovirus has a **single capsid layer**.
- **A packaged RNA polymerase, a matrix layer, a helical nucleocapsid, or nucleoprotein beads strung along the RNA** — the interior holds the bare RNA plus VPg and a few VP2 copies, nothing else.
- **A nucleus, ribosomes, mitochondria, cytoplasm or any cell organelle**, and **no cell wall, flagellum, pilus or capsule** — a virion is not a cell.
- **A bacteriophage head-and-tail body, tail fibres, base plate or legs.**
- **Anything at rotavirus/influenza scale** — beside other viruses it must read as clearly among the smallest.
- **A face, eyes or anthropomorphism** (except in the separate kids' coloring page), and **no text, letters, numbers, labels, arrows, scale bars or watermarks** baked into the image.
- **No black border, frame, vignette, letterbox bar, mat or drop shadow**, and never the artwork drawn as a sheet/card lying on a surface.
