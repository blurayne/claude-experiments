## 1. Scientific reference (the yardstick for verification)

Rotavirus is the textbook example of a **triple-layered, non-enveloped icosahedral virus**. The infectious particle — the **triple-layered particle (TLP)** — is built from **three concentric protein shells** and has, per the ICTV report, an **overall diameter of about 100 nm** once the spikes are counted; the smooth outer rim itself measures **~75-76.5 nm** (the CDC's own negative-stain plate is captioned "the rotavirus is not enveloped, and measures 76.5 nm in diameter"), the **double-layered particle (DLP)** left after the outer coat is stripped is **~70.5 nm**, and the bare **core ~51 nm**. There is **no lipid envelope anywhere** — this is the single most important thing to get right about the particle.

The genus name comes from **Latin *rota*, "wheel"**: [Flewett and colleagues proposed it in *The Lancet* in 1974](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736%2874%2991631-6) after looking at negatively stained stool specimens, in which the sharp outer rim with **short, blunt spikes radiating from a lighter hub-like centre** makes each virion read like a **spoked wheel**. That is exactly the visual signature the renders must reproduce: a crisp circular rim, short stubby projections, and a layered rather than featureless interior — never the long club-headed spikes of a coronavirus ([Etymologia: Rotavirus, *Emerg Infect Dis* 2012](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3381537/)).

Working outward from the middle: the **inner core shell is VP2**, **120 copies** on a **T=1 lattice** (60 asymmetric dimers), and it encloses the genome. Tethered to the inner face of that shell, at the **five-fold vertices**, sit up to **12 copies each of VP1, the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase, and VP3, the capping enzyme** (guanylyl-/methyltransferase), as heterodimeric complexes — rotavirus is a double-stranded RNA virus, so unlike a picornavirus it **must** carry its own polymerase inside. Around VP2 lies the **intermediate layer of VP6**: **780 copies arranged as 260 trimers** on a **T=13 lattice** (20 trimers on the icosahedral three-fold axes, 240 on local three-folds). VP6 is the highly conserved, highly immunogenic **group/species antigen** that defines rotavirus species A–J, and VP2+VP6 together form the **transcriptionally active DLP**.

The outermost layer is **VP7**, a **calcium-dependent glycoprotein**, **780 copies as 260 trimers** on the same **T=13 lattice**, capping the VP6 pillars and gripping them with amino-terminal arms; the trimers are held together by **Ca²⁺** in the inter-subunit interfaces, which is why chelating calcium strips the outer layer and converts a TLP into a DLP. Piercing that shell are the **60 VP4 spikes** — each spike is anchored as a **trimer** in a channel between VP7 and VP6, but only two arms project, giving the characteristic **bilobed** profile above the surface. VP4 must be **cleaved by trypsin in the gut** into **VP8\*** (~28 kDa, the sialic-acid/HBGA-binding head) and **VP5\*** (~60 kDa, the membrane-interacting body) before the virion is infectious; primed spikes are **about 20 nm long and project about 12 nm** beyond the VP7 surface. VP7 defines **G types**, VP4 defines **P types**.

Because both outer layers share **T=13** symmetry, the shell is perforated by a characteristic set of **132 aqueous channels** running from the outer surface down to the VP2 layer. They come in three classes: **12 type-I channels at the icosahedral five-fold axes** — the narrow pores through which **nascent mRNA is extruded** during transcription — plus **60 type-II** channels ringing them and **60 type-III** channels around the three-fold axes ([Aiyegbo et al., *J Virol* 88:469, 2014](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jvi.01665-13)). No turrets sit on the vertices, unlike orthoreovirus.

The genome is **segmented**: **11 discrete linear double-stranded RNA segments**, ranging from **667 to 3,302 nt** and totalling about **18.5 kbp** in the reference SA11 strain, encoding **12 proteins** (six structural VP, six non-structural NSP). The segments sit as a tightly spooled bundle inside the VP2 core, each associated with a VP1/VP3 complex at a vertex. Segmentation is what allows **reassortment** between co-infecting strains — the same trick influenza plays — and it is why the picture must show **eleven separate pieces of dsRNA, not one continuous thread**. The mRNAs made inside the particle are **5'-capped but lack a poly(A) tail**, ending instead in a conserved **UGACC**.

Clinically: rotavirus infects the mature enterocytes of the small-intestinal villi, spreads by the **faecal-oral route**, is shed in enormous numbers, survives well on hands and surfaces, and is the leading cause of severe dehydrating diarrhoea in infants and young children worldwide; live oral vaccines (Rotarix, RotaTeq) have sharply reduced hospitalisations where they are used.

Sources: [ICTV Report — genus *Rotavirus* (*Sedoreoviridae*)](https://ictv.global/report/chapter/sedoreoviridae/sedoreoviridae/rotavirus), [ViralZone / Expasy — Rotavirus](https://viralzone.expasy.org/107), [Settembre et al., "Atomic model of an infectious rotavirus particle", *EMBO J* 30:408 (2011)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3025467/), [Chen et al., "Rotavirus architecture at subnanometer resolution", *J Virol* 83:1754 (2009)](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jvi.01855-08), [Aiyegbo et al., *J Virol* 88:469 (2014) — type I/II/III channels](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jvi.01665-13), [Etymologia: Rotavirus, *Emerg Infect Dis* 18:1029 (2012)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3381537/), [CDC PHIL #197 caption (76.5 nm, non-enveloped)](https://phil.cdc.gov/Details.aspx?pid=197).

### Do NOT draw (scientifically misleading)

- **A lipid envelope or membrane bilayer of any kind** — rotavirus is **non-enveloped**. Wrapping the particle in a soap-bubble membrane is the single most serious error here.
- **Long club-shaped or mushroom-headed spikes, a corona of clubs, HA/NA-style projections or a fuzzy fringe** — the VP4 spikes are **short, blunt and bilobed** (~12 nm proud of a ~75 nm particle), not a coronavirus corona.
- **A helical, rod-shaped, filamentous or bullet-shaped capsid** — the particle is **icosahedral**.
- **A single smooth featureless sphere with no layers** — the whole point is the **three concentric shells** plus the wheel rim and its spokes.
- **Single-stranded RNA, DNA, or a B-form double helix with drawn base-pair rungs** — the genome is **double-stranded RNA**, drawn as paired rods, not as a DNA helix.
- **One single unsegmented genome molecule** — there must be **eleven separate dsRNA segments**.
- **Turrets at the vertices, or a bacteriophage tail, base plate, collar or legs** — rotavirus has no turrets (unlike orthoreovirus) and no tail.
- **A nucleus, ribosomes, mitochondria, cytoplasm or any cell organelle** — a virion is not a cell.
- **A face, eyes, limbs or any anthropomorphism** (except in the separate kids' coloring page), and **no text, letters, numbers, labels, scale bars, arrows or watermarks**.

**Adults (popular science, health).**  
🇬🇧 Rotavirus is the classic cause of severe watery diarrhoea in infants and young children. Before vaccination it infected essentially every child worldwide by the age of five and killed on the order of half a million of them each year, almost all through dehydration rather than through the infection itself. The virion is a non-enveloped, triple-layered particle about 75 nm across whose short surface spikes give it the spoked-wheel look that earned it the name. It infects the mature enterocytes on the tips of the villi in the small intestine — the cells that do the absorbing — and causes diarrhoea by three routes at once: those cells are destroyed and replaced by immature ones that absorb poorly, a viral protein called NSP4 acts as an enterotoxin and drives chloride and water secretion, and the enteric nervous system is stimulated to speed everything along. Because the particle has no lipid envelope it is remarkably durable on hands, toys and surfaces, resists many disinfectants, and is shed in enormous numbers, so hygiene alone never controlled it. Two live oral vaccines given in the first months of life changed the picture completely: countries that introduced them saw hospital admissions for childhood gastroenteritis fall by roughly two thirds within a few years, and the seasonal winter peak flattened. There is no useful antiviral; treatment is rehydration, ideally with low-osmolarity oral rehydration solution, with intravenous fluids reserved for children who cannot keep anything down. Feeding should continue rather than be stopped, and zinc supplementation shortens the illness in settings where deficiency is common. Adults are not immune — they simply tend to get a milder illness, and they pass it on.  
🇩🇪 Rotaviren sind der klassische Erreger schwerer wässriger Durchfälle bei Säuglingen und Kleinkindern. Vor Einführung der Impfung infizierte sich praktisch jedes Kind weltweit bis zum fünften Geburtstag, und jährlich starben in der Größenordnung von einer halben Million Kinder daran — fast alle an der Austrocknung, nicht an der Infektion selbst. Da dem Partikel die Lipidhülle fehlt, hält es sich bemerkenswert lange auf Händen, Spielzeug und Oberflächen, widersteht vielen Desinfektionsmitteln und wird in gewaltigen Mengen ausgeschieden — Hygiene allein hat es deshalb nie in den Griff bekommen. **Scientific.**  
🇬🇧 Rotavirus (family Sedoreoviridae, formerly Reoviridae; genus Rotavirus, species Rotavirus A-J, with group/species A responsible for nearly all human disease) is a non-enveloped, icosahedral, triple-layered particle (TLP) of about 75 nm. The genome is segmented: eleven segments of double-stranded RNA, roughly 18.5 kb in total, each segment encoding one protein except segment 11. Three concentric protein shells enclose it. The innermost core is a T=1 layer of 120 copies of VP2, which packages the eleven dsRNA segments together with the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase VP1 and the capping enzyme VP3, one transcription complex tethered beneath each five-fold vertex. The intermediate layer is 260 trimers of VP6 in T=13 laevo symmetry; VP6 carries the group and subgroup antigens and is the target of most diagnostic immunoassays. The outer layer is 780 copies of the glycoprotein VP7 (T=13), from which 60 spikes of the protease-sensitive protein VP4 project; trypsin cleavage of VP4 into VP8* and VP5* is required for infectivity and yields the rigid, blunt spikes that produce the wheel-like negative-stain profile. VP7 (G-type) and VP4 (P-type) define the binary G/P genotype nomenclature and carry the neutralising epitopes. Aqueous channels of three classes penetrate the VP7 and VP6 layers and permit import of nucleotides and export of nascent mRNA. The dsRNA genome is never released into the cytoplasm: loss of the outer layer during entry produces a transcriptionally active double-layered particle (DLP) in which VP1 transcribes capped, non-polyadenylated plus-strand RNAs that are extruded through the type-I channels. Replication occurs in cytoplasmic viroplasms organised by NSP2 and NSP5, and progeny DLPs bud through the endoplasmic reticulum membrane in an NSP4-dependent step, transiently acquiring and then losing a lipid envelope before the mature TLP is formed. NSP4 additionally functions as a viral enterotoxin, elevating intracellular calcium and triggering age-dependent chloride secretion; together with destruction of mature villus-tip enterocytes, resulting disaccharidase deficiency and enteric nervous system activation, it produces the characteristic secretory-plus-malabsorptive diarrhoea. Segment reassortment between co-infecting strains is frequent and underpins both the diversity of circulating G/P combinations and the design of the reassortant vaccine RotaTeq; Rotarix is instead an attenuated human G1P[8] monovalent strain.  
🇩🇪 Rotaviren (Familie Sedoreoviridae, früher Reoviridae; Gattung Rotavirus, Spezies Rotavirus A-J, wobei Gruppe bzw. Spezies A nahezu alle Erkrankungen des Menschen verursacht) sind unbehüllte, ikosaedrische, dreischichtige Partikel (triple-layered particle, TLP) von etwa 75 nm. Das Genom ist segmentiert: elf Segmente doppelsträngiger RNA mit zusammen rund 18,5 kb, wobei jedes Segment mit Ausnahme von Segment 11 für ein Protein codiert. Drei konzentrische Proteinschalen umschließen es. Der innerste Kern ist eine T=1-Schicht aus 120 Kopien VP2, die die elf dsRNA-Segmente zusammen mit der RNA-abhängigen RNA-Polymerase VP1 und dem Capping-Enzym VP3 verpackt — je ein Transkriptionskomplex unter jedem fünfzähligen Vertex. Die mittlere Schicht besteht aus 260 VP6-Trimeren in T=13-laevo-Symmetrie; VP6 trägt die Gruppen- und Subgruppenantigene und ist die Zielstruktur der meisten diagnostischen Immunoassays. Die äußere Schicht bilden 780 Kopien des Glykoproteins VP7 (T=13), aus der 60 Spikes des proteasesensitiven Proteins VP4 herausragen; die Spaltung von VP4 durch Trypsin in VP8* und VP5* ist Voraussetzung für die Infektiosität und erzeugt jene starren, stumpfen Fortsätze, die im Negativkontrast das radförmige Profil ergeben. VP7 (G-Typ) und VP4 (P-Typ) definieren die binäre G/P-Genotypisierung und tragen die neutralisierenden Epitope. Wässrige Kanäle dreier Klassen durchziehen die VP7- und VP6-Schicht und erlauben den Import von Nukleotiden sowie den Export naszierender mRNA. Das dsRNA-Genom wird niemals ins Zytoplasma freigesetzt: Der Verlust der äußeren Schicht beim Eintritt erzeugt ein transkriptionsaktives doppelschichtiges Partikel (DLP), in dem VP1 gecappte, nicht polyadenylierte Plusstrang-RNAs synthetisiert, die durch die Typ-I-Kanäle ausgeschleust werden. Die Replikation läuft in zytoplasmatischen Viroplasmen ab, die von NSP2 und NSP5 organisiert werden; Tochter-DLPs knospen in einem NSP4-abhängigen Schritt in das Lumen des endoplasmatischen Retikulums, erhalten dabei vorübergehend eine Lipidhülle und verlieren sie wieder, bevor das reife TLP entsteht. NSP4 wirkt zusätzlich als virales Enterotoxin, erhöht das intrazelluläre Calcium und löst eine altersabhängige Chloridsekretion aus; zusammen mit der Zerstörung reifer Enterozyten an den Zottenspitzen, dem daraus folgenden Disaccharidasemangel und der Aktivierung des enterischen Nervensystems entsteht die charakteristische sekretorisch-malabsorptive Diarrhö. Reassortment einzelner Segmente zwischen koinfizierenden Stämmen ist häufig und erklärt sowohl die Vielfalt zirkulierender G/P-Kombinationen als auch das Konstruktionsprinzip des Reassortanten-Impfstoffs RotaTeq; Rotarix ist demgegenüber ein attenuierter humaner monovalenter G1P[8]-Stamm.
