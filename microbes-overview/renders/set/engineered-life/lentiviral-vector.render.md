## 1. Scientific reference (the yardstick for verification)

A lentiviral vector particle is a **laboratory-manufactured, replication-incompetent HIV-1 derivative**, not a pathogen. It is built by transient co-transfection of a producer cell (typically HEK293T) with **separate plasmids**: a *transfer plasmid* carrying only the cargo gene flanked by **self-inactivating long terminal repeats (SIN-LTRs)**, plus the packaging signal (Psi), RRE and PPT; a *gag-pol plasmid*; a *rev plasmid*; and an *envelope plasmid*, almost always **VSV-G** (vesicular stomatitis virus glycoprotein) rather than HIV's own Env. The proteins these plasmids encode assemble a physically normal-looking HIV-1-architecture particle around the transfer-plasmid RNA — but that packaged RNA itself encodes none of the proteins needed to build another particle. That is the mechanism of "replication-incompetent," and it cannot be drawn as an absence; it has to be taught through what the particle's genome cargo actually is (a single transgene, not viral genes) plus explicit labels.

**Gross morphology — identical scale and architecture to HIV-1:** enveloped, roughly spherical, **~100–120 nm** in diameter (about 1/1000th the diameter of the T cell it is typically used to modify). The outer layer is a **host-derived lipid bilayer envelope**. A thin **matrix protein (Gag-derived, MA/p17)** shell lines the inside of the envelope. At the centre is the diagnostic **conical (cone/bullet-shaped) capsid**, built from HIV-1 Gag capsid protein (CA/p24) — wide at one end, tapering to a narrower tip, the lentiviral signature, NOT spherical and NOT icosahedral. Inside the cone: a single-stranded RNA transcript coated by nucleocapsid protein (NC/p7), plus the virion enzymes **reverse transcriptase** and **integrase** (both Pol products, packaged into the particle just as in wild-type HIV).

**What is different from wild-type HIV-1, and why each difference matters for the picture:**
- **Envelope glycoprotein — VSV-G pseudotype, not gp120/gp41.** Most research and clinical lentiviral vectors are pseudotyped with the vesicular stomatitis virus G glycoprotein because it gives broad cell tropism and lets the particle survive ultracentrifugation (unlike HIV's own Env). VSV-G trimers coat the envelope in a **dense, fairly uniform layer** — this is a real, citable, and labellable difference from HIV's own envelope, whose gp120/gp41 trimers are famously **sparse** (~7–14 per virion). Render the vector's spike coat as visibly denser and more evenly distributed than the `hiv` key's render, not the same sparse scatter.
- **Genome cargo — a therapeutic/marker transgene, not the viral genome.** The single RNA strand inside the capsid carries **one cargo gene (transgene) flanked by SIN-LTRs** plus the minimal cis-acting elements (Psi, RRE, PPT) — not gag, pol, env, tat, rev, vif, vpr, vpu or nef. Only ~30% or less of the transfer-plasmid sequence is even HIV-derived.
- **SIN-LTRs.** The 3′ LTR carries a deletion in its promoter/enhancer that is copied to the 5′ LTR during reverse transcription, so the integrated provirus cannot drive its own transcription — a second, independent layer of the "cannot make more of itself" property, and one that IS drawable as a labelled feature (mark the two ends of the RNA cassette where the LTRs sit).
- **No accessory/regulatory proteins packaged as functional genes** — tat is eliminated entirely in third-generation systems (an internal constitutive promoter replaces its role) and vif/vpr/vpu/nef are simply never included on any plasmid.

Sources: [Dull et al. 1998, *J Virol* — "A third-generation lentivirus vector with a conditional packaging system"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC109514/), [Naldini et al. 1996, *Science* — original VSV-G-pseudotyped HIV-1-derived vector](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8602510/), [Milone & O'Doherty 2018, *Leukemia* — "Clinical use of lentiviral vectors"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6035154/), [Addgene — Lentiviral Vector Guide](https://www.addgene.org/guides/lentivirus/), [Cornell EHS — Lentiviral Vectors (3rd Generation+) Biological Agent Reference Sheet](https://ehs.cornell.edu/research-safety/biosafety-biosecurity/biological-safety-manuals-and-other-documents/bars-other/lentiviral-vectors-3rd-generation), plus the morphology baseline already verified for the `hiv` key ([ViralZone HIV-1](https://viralzone.expasy.org/5061), [PDB-101 HIV capsid](https://pdb101.rcsb.org/motm/141)).

### Composition decision for "replication-incompetent" (per style)
A picture cannot show an absence directly, so this figure uses **two honest channels together** rather than inventing a visual gimmick (no crossed-out symbol, no "broken" particle — the house style is anatomy, not iconography):
1. **textbook / 3d / watercolor** use a **cutaway**, because one is needed anyway to show the internal capsid architecture, revealing the single RNA cargo cassette (not a multi-gene viral genome map) between its two SIN-LTR ends, plus reverse transcriptase and integrase — the same physical layout as `hiv`'s cutaway, but with a visibly simpler, single-cassette interior.
2. **Labels on every annotated style** carry the vocabulary that makes the meaning explicit: *cargo gene (transgene)*, *SIN-LTR*, *VSV-G envelope glycoprotein*, *reverse transcriptase*, *integrase*, *conical capsid (Gag)*. The label text is doing real teaching work here that the silhouette alone cannot.
3. **sem** (false-colour surface-only, no cutaway, no labels per house rule) carries only the VSV-G-density difference from `hiv`'s sparse-spike SEM — correctly incomplete on its own, which is why sem is not asked to carry "replication-incompetent" at all.
The description text (func/deps, already written) and the reference-image caption below carry the rest: what "replication-incompetent" means and why it cannot be photographed.

### Do NOT draw (scientifically misleading)
- **Icosahedral / geometric-ball capsid** — must be the **cone (bullet)**, wide-to-narrow, exactly as in `hiv`.
- **HIV's own sparse mushroom-shaped gp120/gp41 spikes** — this is the VSV-G pseudotype: draw a **denser, more uniform** trimeric spike coat, visibly different from the `hiv` render's sparse ~10-spike scatter, not the same sparse pattern.
- **A crossed-out symbol, an "X", a broken/cracked particle, or any other iconographic marker for "cannot replicate"** — the house style is anatomy; the meaning is carried by the cutaway + labels, not a gimmick.
- **A multi-gene viral genome map inside the capsid** — only ONE simple RNA cassette (cargo gene between two LTR ends), not gag/pol/env/accessory genes drawn as separate labelled segments.
- **DNA double helix inside the particle** — still an RNA cargo pre-integration, same as wild-type HIV.
- **Bacteriophage tail / legs / lander, bacterial anatomy, a face or eyes, or any anthropomorphism.**
- **The HIV virion itself being rendered here** — this is the *vector*; `hiv` already exists as its own subject and is cross-referenced, not re-rendered.

## 3. Audience descriptions (EN + DE)

**Kids (GiantMicrobes-style).**  
🇬🇧 Meet the gene-delivery van! Scientists started with HIV, a virus, and stripped out almost everything that made it a virus — keeping only its delivery-truck shape and the key it uses to unlock a cell's door. Then they loaded it with one helpful gene instead of virus instructions, so now all it can do is drive up to a cell, unlock the door, and drop its package inside. It cannot make copies of itself and it cannot make anyone sick — it only gets to deliver its package once. Doctors use it to drop a new gene into cells that need fixing, like teaching a patient's own defender cells a clever new trick for fighting cancer (see CAR-T cell).  
🇩🇪 Das ist der Gen-Lieferwagen! Forschende haben HIV genommen, ein Virus, und fast alles entfernt, was es zu einem Virus macht — übrig blieb nur die Form des Lieferwagens und der Schlüssel, mit dem er die Tür einer Zelle aufschließt. Dann haben sie ihn mit einem einzigen hilfreichen Gen beladen statt mit Virus-Bauplänen. Jetzt kann er nur noch bei einer Zelle vorfahren, die Tür aufschließen und sein Paket abliefern. Er kann sich nicht vervielfältigen und niemanden krank machen — er darf sein Paket nur ein einziges Mal abliefern. Ärztinnen und Ärzte nutzen ihn, um ein neues Gen in Zellen zu bringen, die repariert werden müssen — zum Beispiel, um den körpereigenen Abwehrzellen eines Patienten einen cleveren neuen Trick gegen Krebs beizubringen (siehe CAR-T-Zelle).

**Adults (popular science, health).**  
🇬🇧 The lentiviral vector is a laboratory-built delivery vehicle assembled from HIV-1 parts, engineered to enter a cell and write a single chosen gene into that cell's DNA — and do nothing else. Its own viral genes have been stripped out and spread across separate manufacturing plasmids that never travel inside the finished particle, so the particle itself carries only the cargo gene, flanked by “self-inactivating” LTR sequences that keep it from switching back on once integrated. It cannot replicate and cannot cause an HIV infection — there is no HIV genome inside it to catch. It is typically used once, to permanently reprogram cells that will rarely or never divide again, most famously a patient's own T cells being converted into CAR-T cancer therapies (see CAR-T cell) or blood stem cells being corrected for an inherited disease.  
🇩🇪 Der lentivirale Vektor ist ein im Labor gebautes Transportfahrzeug aus HIV-1-Bauteilen, konstruiert, um in eine Zelle einzudringen und genau ein ausgewähltes Gen in deren DNS zu schreiben — und sonst nichts. Seine eigenen viralen Gene wurden entfernt und auf getrennte Herstellungs-Plasmide verteilt, die nie im fertigen Partikel landen; das Partikel selbst trägt daher nur das Fracht-Gen, flankiert von „selbstinaktivierenden“ LTR-Sequenzen, die verhindern, dass es sich nach dem Einbau wieder einschaltet. Er kann sich nicht vermehren und keine HIV-Infektion auslösen — es steckt kein HIV-Erbgut darin, das man sich einfangen könnte. Meist wird er nur einmal eingesetzt, um Zellen dauerhaft umzuprogrammieren, die sich selten oder nie wieder teilen — am bekanntesten die körpereigenen T-Zellen eines Patienten, die zu CAR-T-Krebstherapien werden (siehe CAR-T-Zelle), oder korrigierte Blutstammzellen bei einer Erbkrankheit.

**Scientific.**  
🇬🇧 A lentiviral vector is a replication-incompetent HIV-1-derived particle produced by transient co-transfection of a producer cell with separate plasmids: a transfer plasmid carrying only the transgene flanked by self-inactivating (SIN) LTRs plus the minimal cis-acting elements (Psi packaging signal, RRE, PPT); a gag-pol plasmid; a rev plasmid; and, in almost all modern systems, a VSV-G envelope plasmid in place of HIV's own Env, which broadens tropism and confers the physical stability needed for ultracentrifugal concentration. The assembled particle is structurally indistinguishable from wild-type HIV-1 — ~100–120 nm, enveloped, with a conical Gag-derived (p24) capsid enclosing the RNA genome, reverse transcriptase and integrase — but the RNA it packages encodes no viral proteins, only the cargo gene; tat is eliminated entirely in third-generation systems and the accessory genes vif, vpr, vpu and nef are never included on any plasmid. After VSV-G-mediated entry, reverse transcriptase converts the RNA to double-stranded DNA and integrase inserts it into the host genome, where the SIN-LTR deletion — copied from the 3' to the 5' LTR during reverse transcription — disables the LTR's own promoter/enhancer activity, preventing reactivation of the integrated cassette. Because no plasmid contributes packaging-competent viral genes to the transduced cell, the vector cannot generate infectious progeny.  
🇩🇪 Ein lentiviraler Vektor ist ein replikationsunfähiges, von HIV-1 abgeleitetes Partikel, hergestellt durch transiente Kotransfektion einer Produzentenzelle mit getrennten Plasmiden: einem Transferplasmid, das nur das Transgen zwischen selbstinaktivierenden (SIN-)LTRs sowie die minimalen cis-aktiven Elemente (Psi-Verpackungssignal, RRE, PPT) trägt; einem Gag-Pol-Plasmid; einem Rev-Plasmid; und, in nahezu allen modernen Systemen, einem VSV-G-Hüllplasmid anstelle von HIVs eigenem Env, was den Zelltropismus erweitert und die physikalische Stabilität für die Aufkonzentration per Ultrazentrifugation verleiht. Das zusammengesetzte Partikel ist strukturell nicht von Wildtyp-HIV-1 zu unterscheiden — rund 100–120 nm, behüllt, mit einem konischen, von Gag abgeleiteten (p24) Kapsid, das das RNS-Genom, Reverse Transkriptase und Integrase umschließt — doch die verpackte RNS kodiert keine viralen Proteine, sondern nur das Fracht-Gen; tat entfällt in Systemen der dritten Generation vollständig, und die akzessorischen Gene vif, vpr, vpu und nef sind auf keinem der Plasmide enthalten. Nach dem VSV-G-vermittelten Eintritt wandelt die Reverse Transkriptase die RNS in doppelsträngige DNS um, und die Integrase baut sie ins Wirtsgenom ein, wo die SIN-LTR-Deletion — während der reversen Transkription von der 3'- auf die 5'-LTR kopiert — die Promotor-/Enhancer-Aktivität der LTR selbst stilllegt und so eine Reaktivierung der integrierten Kassette verhindert. Da kein Plasmid verpackungskompetente virale Gene in die transduzierte Zelle einbringt, kann der Vektor keine infektiösen Nachkommen erzeugen.

Feorino, 1989): colorized TEM of 2-3 clearly resolved, isolated mature HIV-1 virions (magenta envelope, dense dark core) against a uniform green background; normalised 1080x1080 crop has no baked-in text, caption, scale bar or border. This is an HIV-1 plate, NOT a lentiviral-vector-specific image — no freely-licensed vector-specific micrograph could be found (Wikimedia Commons, CDC PHIL, NIAID/NIH Flickr and general web search all checked; the one vector-specific TEM figure found, in Molecular Therapy, is CC BY-NC-ND and was excluded on licence grounds). Disclosed explicitly in the caption per the chlamydia-felis precedent. Free/mature (non-budding) particles were chosen deliberately over a budding-from-cell scene as the more honest visual analogue for a purified, manufactured vector product.
