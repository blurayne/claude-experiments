## 1. Scientific reference (the yardstick for verification)

The Sertoli cell (sustentacular cell) is the somatic "nurse" cell of the seminiferous tubule in the testis. Unlike a round blood cell or a simple cuboidal epithelial cell, it is a tall, irregular, pyramidal-to-columnar cell that rests with a broad base on the tubule's basal lamina and extends elaborate, branching apical and lateral cytoplasmic processes all the way up to the lumen, so that in cross-section a single Sertoli cell's outline is highly irregular — full of deep pockets and recesses that cradle developing germ cells (spermatogonia, spermatocytes, spermatids) at successive stages. The nucleus sits basally, is typically oval to triangular/pear-shaped with a deeply infolded ("grooved") nuclear envelope and pale, euchromatic chromatin, and contains a characteristic large, tripartite nucleolus (a central pars amorpha flanked by two pars granulosa masses) — a helpful diagnostic feature distinct from the small, round, dark nuclei of adjacent germ cells. The cytoplasm is rich in smooth endoplasmic reticulum (supporting steroid/hormone handling and secretion of inhibin and androgen-binding protein), with comparatively scant rough ER, a well-developed Golgi apparatus, numerous elongated mitochondria, and lipid droplets (their number varies with the phase of the spermatogenic cycle, reflecting phagocytosis of residual bodies shed by maturing spermatids). Human Sertoli cells also characteristically contain Charcot-Böttcher crystalloids — elongated, rod-shaped proteinaceous inclusions several micrometres long, formed by aggregation of intermediate (vimentin) filaments; their function is unclear but their presence is a distinctive, teachable feature of this cell type. An extensive cytoskeleton of vimentin intermediate filaments (forming a basket around the nucleus and running the height of the cell) and microtubules give the cell its tall shape and structural support, while actin-rich junctional plaques (ectoplasmic specializations) anchor the cell to neighbouring germ cells and Sertoli cells. Near the base, adjacent Sertoli cells are joined by extensive tight junctions (zonulae occludentes) that divide the epithelium into a basal compartment (spermatogonia) and an adluminal compartment (meiotic/post-meiotic germ cells) — this is the anatomical basis of the blood-testis barrier, which keeps the immune system from seeing the antigenically "foreign" haploid germ cells and maintains the special ionic/hormonal microenvironment germ cells need to mature. Sertoli cells respond to FSH from the pituitary (via FSH receptors) and to testosterone from neighbouring Leydig cells, and in turn steer germ-cell maturation and control its pace by secreting inhibin, androgen-binding protein (ABP) and other signalling molecules; they do not divide once mature and one Sertoli cell supports a fixed, limited number of germ cells, which is why Sertoli cell number set in early life caps adult sperm output.

Sources: [Wikipedia — Sertoli cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sertoli_cell), [StatPearls — Histology, Sertoli Cell (NCBI Bookshelf)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560631/), [Nakazato & Ogawa — Histochemical and electron microscopic studies of Charcot-Böttcher crystalloids in human Sertoli cells](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/HISTOCHEMICAL-AND-ELECTRON-MICROSCOPIC-STUDIES-OF-Nakazato-Ogawa/782378a694a6beb4e87b52e4a9c382e57b03db59), [ScienceDirect Topics — Sertoli Cell](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/medicine-and-dentistry/sertoli-cell), [Histology at SIU — Male reproductive system, Sertoli cells](https://histology.siu.edu/erg/RE028b.htm).

### Do NOT draw (scientifically misleading)
- **No cell wall** — this is an animal cell, not a plant cell or bacterium.
- **No nucleoid, plasmids or bacterial-style flagella** — not a prokaryote.
- **No chloroplasts, no large central vacuole** — those are plant-cell features.
- **Not round/spherical** — the defining shape is a TALL, irregular, pyramidal-to-columnar cell spanning the full height of the seminiferous epithelium (base to lumen), with deep lateral recesses — not a compact round or cuboidal cell.
- **No eukaryotic flagellum/axoneme on the Sertoli cell itself** — that belongs to the sperm it nurses, not to this cell; do not draw a tail.
- **No germ cells crowding/filling the frame** — this is a single-specimen render of the Sertoli cell; small empty recesses/pockets in its outline may hint at where germ cells would nestle, but do not populate them with other cell bodies.
- **Nucleolus should read as a single large, complex (tripartite) body**, not multiple small dot-like nucleoli scattered around — that reads as a different cell type.
- A single specimen, not a dense confluent epithelial sheet — individual morphology must stay readable.

**Adults (popular science, health).**  
🇬🇧 The Sertoli cell is the testis's dedicated support cell: a tall, irregularly branching structure that spans the full height of the seminiferous tubule wall, from the outer basal lamina to the central lumen. Along its length it cradles developing sperm cells in deep cytoplasmic pockets, nourishing them and guiding them through the stages of maturation. Tight junctions between neighbouring Sertoli cells form the blood-testis barrier, a physical seal that keeps the bloodstream (and immune system) separate from the more advanced, genetically distinct germ cells, and that maintains the special fluid environment sperm need to develop properly. Sertoli cells respond to pituitary FSH and to testosterone from nearby Leydig cells, and in turn secrete inhibin (which feeds back to the pituitary to help regulate FSH levels) and androgen-binding protein (which keeps local testosterone concentrated where it's needed). Because each Sertoli cell can only support a fixed number of germ cells, the number of Sertoli cells a person has, largely set before and around puberty, puts a ceiling on lifetime sperm-production capacity.  
🇩🇪 Die Sertoli-Zelle ist die eigens zuständige Stützzelle des Hodens: eine hohe, unregelmäßig verzweigte Struktur, die die gesamte Höhe der Hodenkanälchen-Wand überspannt, von der äußeren Basallamina bis zum zentralen Lumen. Entlang ihrer Länge hält sie heranreifende Spermien in tiefen Zytoplasma-Taschen, ernährt sie und begleitet sie durch die Reifungsstufen. Tight Junctions zwischen benachbarten Sertoli-Zellen bilden die Blut-Hoden-Schranke, eine physische Abdichtung, die den Blutkreislauf (und das Immunsystem) von den weiter entwickelten, genetisch andersartigen Keimzellen trennt und die besondere Flüssigkeitsumgebung aufrechterhält, die Spermien zur richtigen Entwicklung brauchen. Sertoli-Zellen reagieren auf FSH aus der Hypophyse und auf Testosteron aus benachbarten Leydig-Zellen und geben ihrerseits Inhibin ab (das über eine Rückkopplung an die Hypophyse den FSH-Spiegel mitregelt) sowie Androgen-bindendes Protein (das lokal Testosteron dort konzentriert hält, wo es gebraucht wird). Da jede Sertoli-Zelle nur eine feste Anzahl an Keimzellen versorgen kann, setzt die Zahl der Sertoli-Zellen, die größtenteils schon vor und um die Pubertät festgelegt wird, eine Obergrenze für die lebenslange Spermienproduktion.

**Scientific.**  
🇬🇧 The Sertoli cell (sustentacular cell) is the somatic epithelial cell of the seminiferous tubule, extending from the basal lamina to the tubular lumen and enveloping germ cells at all stages of spermatogenesis within elaborate lateral and apical cytoplasmic recesses. Ultrastructurally it is characterized by a basally located, irregularly infolded euchromatic nucleus with a prominent tripartite nucleolus, abundant smooth endoplasmic reticulum, comparatively scant rough ER, a well-developed Golgi apparatus, numerous elongated mitochondria, variable numbers of lipid droplets reflecting phagocytosis of residual bodies, and, in humans, characteristic Charcot-Böttcher crystalloids formed by aggregation of vimentin intermediate filaments. Extensive vimentin filament and microtubule networks provide structural support along the cell's basal-apical axis, while actin-based ectoplasmic specializations mediate junctional attachments to germ cells and neighbouring Sertoli cells. Occluding (tight) junctions between adjacent Sertoli cells near the basal lamina constitute the blood-testis barrier, subdividing the epithelium into basal and adluminal compartments, restricting paracellular permeability, and sequestering the meiotic and post-meiotic germ cells from systemic immune surveillance. Sertoli cells express FSH receptors and androgen receptors, integrating endocrine input from the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis and paracrine testosterone from Leydig cells to regulate spermatogenesis via secreted factors including inhibin B, androgen-binding protein, and stem-cell factor; being post-mitotic in the adult, their fixed number, established during the perinatal and pubertal proliferative windows, is a principal determinant of adult testicular sperm-production capacity.  
🇩🇪 Die Sertoli-Zelle (Stützzelle, sustentakuläre Zelle) ist die somatische Epithelzelle des Hodenkanälchens; sie erstreckt sich von der Basallamina bis zum Tubuluslumen und umhüllt Keimzellen aller Reifungsstadien in ausgedehnten lateralen und apikalen Zytoplasma-Nischen. Ultrastrukturell ist sie gekennzeichnet durch einen basal gelegenen, unregelmäßig eingefalteten euchromatischen Zellkern mit ausgeprägtem, dreiteiligem Nukleolus, reichlich glattem endoplasmatischem Retikulum, vergleichsweise spärlichem rauem ER, einem gut entwickelten Golgi-Apparat, zahlreichen langgestreckten Mitochondrien, einer variablen Anzahl an Lipidtröpfchen (Ausdruck der Phagozytose abgestoßener Residualkörperchen) sowie, beim Menschen, den charakteristischen Charcot-Böttcher-Kristalloiden, die durch Aggregation von Vimentin-Intermediärfilamenten entstehen. Ausgedehnte Vimentin-Filament- und Mikrotubuli-Netzwerke stützen die Zelle entlang ihrer basal-apikalen Achse, während aktinbasierte ektoplasmatische Spezialisierungen die junktionalen Verbindungen zu Keimzellen und benachbarten Sertoli-Zellen vermitteln. Verschlusskontakte (Tight Junctions) zwischen benachbarten Sertoli-Zellen nahe der Basallamina bilden die Blut-Hoden-Schranke, die das Epithel in ein basales und ein adluminales Kompartiment unterteilt, die parazelluläre Permeabilität einschränkt und die meiotischen sowie postmeiotischen Keimzellen der systemischen Immunüberwachung entzieht. Sertoli-Zellen exprimieren FSH-Rezeptoren und Androgenrezeptoren, integrieren endokrine Signale der Hypothalamus-Hypophysen-Gonaden-Achse sowie parakrines Testosteron aus Leydig-Zellen und steuern die Spermatogenese über sezernierte Faktoren wie Inhibin B, Androgen-bindendes Protein und Stammzellfaktor; da sie im erwachsenen Organismus postmitotisch sind, bestimmt ihre feste, während der perinatalen und pubertären Proliferationsphasen festgelegte Anzahl maßgeblich die spätere Spermienproduktionskapazität des Hodens.
