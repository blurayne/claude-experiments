## Granulosa cell (`granulosa-cell`)
Small cuboidal-to-columnar epithelial cell that forms the wall of the ovarian follicle around the maturing oocyte, nurses it via gap junctions, and converts theca-cell androgens into estrogen; after ovulation it becomes part of the corpus luteum.

## Oocyte (egg cell) (`oocyte`)
The largest human cell (~100–120 µm), arrested in meiosis II at ovulation, wrapped in the glycoprotein zona pellucida and a corona of cumulus cells; packed with nutrient stores, mitochondria and cortical granules that block polyspermy after fertilisation.

## Sertoli cell (`sertoli-cell`)
Tall, irregular columnar "nurse" cell that spans the seminiferous epithelium from the basal lamina to the tubule lumen, cradles and feeds developing sperm cells in deep lateral recesses, and forms the blood-testis barrier via tight junctions between neighbouring Sertoli cells.

## Spermatozoon (sperm) (`spermatozoon`)
Highly polarized, streamlined male germ cell (~55-60 micrometres long): a flattened oval head carrying the haploid, ultra-condensed nucleus under an enzyme-filled acrosomal cap, a short neck with the centrioles, a mitochondria-wrapped midpiece for ATP, and a long flagellar tail (principal piece + end piece) built around a 9+2 axoneme that drives whip-like swimming toward the egg.

## Theca cell (`theca-cell`)
Steroidogenic stromal cell forming the outer capsule (theca interna + externa) of a growing ovarian follicle; under LH stimulation it makes androgens that the neighbouring granulosa cells aromatize into estrogen.
