## 1. Scientific reference (the yardstick for verification)

Granulosa cells are the somatic epithelial cells that line the wall of the ovarian follicle and surround the developing oocyte throughout folliculogenesis. In the primordial and primary follicle they form a single layer of flattened-to-cuboidal cells resting on a basal lamina that separates them from the surrounding theca and ovarian stroma; as the follicle grows the granulosa layer proliferates into several layers of small, round-to-polygonal cuboidal cells with a high nucleus-to-cytoplasm ratio, a round to oval, often centrally placed nucleus with finely dispersed (euchromatic) chromatin, and typically one or two small nucleoli. As the antral (Graafian) follicle forms, the granulosa population differentiates into two functionally distinct groups: mural granulosa cells, which stay attached to the basal lamina lining the follicle wall, and cumulus granulosa cells (cumulus oophorus / corona radiata), which stay in direct contact with the oocyte and are linked to it and to each other through abundant gap junctions — the physical route by which granulosa cells shuttle nutrients, ions and small signalling molecules to the oocyte and receive oocyte-derived factors (GDF9, BMP15) in return. Ultrastructurally the cytoplasm of a granulosa cell is that of a hard-working, moderately steroidogenic epithelial cell: a well-developed rough endoplasmic reticulum and prominent Golgi apparatus for protein/hormone secretion, an increasing amount of smooth endoplasmic reticulum as the cell differentiates and takes on steroidogenic (estrogen-producing) work, numerous mitochondria with the tubulovesicular ("tubular") cristae characteristic of steroid-hormone-producing cells rather than the flat shelf-like cristae of most other tissues, and scattered lipid droplets that store cholesterol precursor for steroid synthesis. Granulosa cells express follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) receptors on their plasma membrane; FSH binding drives cAMP-mediated signalling that (a) stimulates aromatase (CYP19A1), the enzyme that converts androgens made by the neighbouring theca interna cells (in response to LH) into estrogens — the two-cell, two-gonadotropin model of ovarian estrogen production — and (b) later induces the granulosa cells to also express LH receptors as the follicle approaches ovulation. A classic, easily recognised histological feature of a maturing antral follicle is the Call-Exner body: a small, round, eosinophilic (pink on H&E) pool of basal-lamina-like extracellular material surrounded by a rosette of granulosa cells, thought to reflect local basal-lamina-component secretion by the cells; these are a helpful diagnostic feature of granulosa-derived tissue (including granulosa cell tumours). After the LH surge triggers ovulation, the ruptured follicle's mural granulosa cells (together with the theca interna) do not degenerate but instead undergo luteinization: they enlarge, accumulate lipid and lose much of their mitotic capacity, converting into large, pale-staining granulosa-lutein cells that form the bulk of the corpus luteum and switch their steroidogenic output from mainly estrogen to mainly progesterone, sustaining the uterine lining until either implantation or, in its absence, the corpus luteum's regression.

Sources: [Wikipedia — Granulosa cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granulosa_cell), [Wikipedia — Membrana granulosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Membrana_granulosa), [StatPearls — Physiology, Ovarian Follicle Development (NCBI Bookshelf)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK539827/), [PMC — Human Granulosa Cells: Stemness Properties, Molecular Cross-Talk and Follicular Angiogenesis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8229878/), [PubMed — Ultrastructure and composition of Call-Exner bodies in bovine follicles](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10382280/), [ScienceDirect Topics — Granulosa Cell](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/immunology-and-microbiology/granulosa-cell), [Histology at SIU — Ovary](https://histology.siu.edu/erg/ovary.htm).

### Do NOT draw (scientifically misleading)
- **No cell wall** — this is an animal cell, not a plant cell or bacterium.
- **No nucleoid, plasmids or bacterial-style flagella/pili** — not a prokaryote.
- **No chloroplasts, no large central vacuole** — those are plant-cell features.
- **Not tall/columnar with branching processes** — unlike a Sertoli cell, a granulosa cell is SMALL, round-to-polygonal and cuboidal, with a high nucleus-to-cytoplasm ratio; do not draw it as an elongated or stellate cell.
- **No eukaryotic flagellum/axoneme or sperm tail** — granulosa cells are non-motile; do not draw a tail or cilium-like appendage.
- **No large lipid-filled vacuole dominating the cell** (that reads as a Leydig cell or adipocyte) — granulosa-cell lipid droplets are present but small and modest, not the defining feature.
- **A Call-Exner body is a small extracellular eosinophilic pool ringed by cells, not an organelle inside a single cell** — if shown, place it correctly at a multi-cell junction (or omit it for a true single-specimen render and mention it only where the composition allows a small cluster).
- **The oocyte itself is a separate, much larger cell — do not draw a large central nucleus/germinal vesicle typical of an oocyte inside what is meant to be a granulosa cell.**
- A single specimen (or a small realistic rosette/cluster for the textbook/3D/watercolor cutaway, per the style prompts), not a dense confluent sheet filling the whole frame — individual cell morphology must stay readable.

**Adults (popular science, health).**  
🇬🇧 Granulosa cells are the small, densely packed cells that line the wall of every ovarian follicle and directly surround the maturing egg. Linked to the oocyte and to each other by gap junctions, they act as its life-support system, shuttling nutrients and signalling molecules back and forth as the egg grows. Their other defining job is hormonal: granulosa cells carry FSH receptors, and in response to the pituitary hormone FSH they switch on an enzyme called aromatase that converts androgens made by neighbouring theca cells into estrogen, making them the ovary's main estrogen factory during the first half of the menstrual cycle. After ovulation, granulosa cells don't disappear; they transform, together with theca cells, into the corpus luteum, a temporary hormone-producing structure that now makes mostly progesterone to prepare and maintain the uterine lining in case of pregnancy.  
🇩🇪 Granulosazellen sind die kleinen, dicht gepackten Zellen, die die Wand jedes Eibläschens (Follikels) auskleiden und die heranreifende Eizelle direkt umgeben. Über Gap Junctions mit der Eizelle und untereinander verbunden, fungieren sie als ihr Lebenserhaltungssystem und leiten Nährstoffe und Botenstoffe hin und her, während die Eizelle heranwächst. Ihre andere zentrale Aufgabe ist hormoneller Natur: Granulosazellen tragen FSH-Rezeptoren, und als Reaktion auf das Hypophysenhormon FSH aktivieren sie ein Enzym namens Aromatase, das Androgene aus benachbarten Theca-Zellen in Östrogen umwandelt — damit sind sie in der ersten Zyklushälfte die wichtigste Östrogenquelle des Eierstocks. Nach dem Eisprung verschwinden Granulosazellen nicht, sondern verwandeln sich zusammen mit den Theca-Zellen in den Gelbkörper (Corpus luteum), eine vorübergehende Hormonstruktur, die nun vor allem Progesteron bildet, um die Gebärmutterschleimhaut auf eine mögliche Schwangerschaft vorzubereiten und zu erhalten.

**Scientific.**  
🇬🇧 Granulosa cells are the somatic epithelial cells of the ovarian follicle, derived from the ovarian surface epithelium/coelomic mesothelium, that surround the oocyte from the primordial-follicle stage onward and proliferate to form the membrana granulosa within a basal lamina separating them from the theca. As antral follicles mature, granulosa cells segregate into mural (basal-lamina-attached) and cumulus (peri-oocyte) subpopulations, the latter coupled to the oocyte via extensive gap junctions that mediate bidirectional metabolic and paracrine exchange (including oocyte-derived GDF9/BMP15 signalling). Ultrastructurally they show a well-developed rough ER and Golgi, increasing smooth ER with differentiation, and mitochondria with tubulovesicular cristae typical of steroidogenic cells. Granulosa cells express FSH receptors that, via cAMP/PKA signalling, upregulate aromatase (CYP19A1), enabling conversion of theca-derived androgens (produced under LH stimulation) into estrogens — the two-cell, two-gonadotropin model of follicular estrogen synthesis — and later induce LH-receptor expression as ovulation approaches. Call-Exner bodies, eosinophilic pools of basal-lamina-like extracellular matrix surrounded by granulosa-cell rosettes, are a characteristic histological marker of granulosa differentiation. Following the LH surge and ovulation, mural granulosa cells undergo luteinization — hypertrophy, loss of proliferative capacity, and a steroidogenic shift toward progesterone — to become granulosa-lutein cells, the principal cell type of the corpus luteum, which sustains the secretory endometrium during the luteal phase pending implantation or, in its absence, undergoes luteolysis.  
🇩🇪 Granulosazellen sind die somatischen Epithelzellen des Ovarialfollikels, abgeleitet vom Oberflächenepithel des Ovars bzw. dem zölomischen Mesothel; sie umgeben die Eizelle ab dem Primordialfollikel-Stadium und proliferieren zur Membrana granulosa innerhalb einer Basallamina, die sie von der Theca trennt. In heranreifenden Antralfollikeln gliedern sich Granulosazellen in eine murale (basallaminanahe) und eine kumuläre (eizellnahe) Subpopulation; letztere ist über ausgedehnte Gap Junctions mit der Eizelle gekoppelt, die einen bidirektionalen metabolischen und parakrinen Austausch vermitteln (einschließlich der oozytären GDF9/BMP15-Signalgebung). Ultrastrukturell zeigen sie ein gut entwickeltes raues ER und Golgi-Apparat sowie mit fortschreitender Differenzierung zunehmend glattes ER und Mitochondrien mit tubulovesikulären Cristae, wie sie für steroidogene Zellen typisch sind. Granulosazellen exprimieren FSH-Rezeptoren, die über cAMP/PKA-Signalwege die Aromatase (CYP19A1) hochregulieren und so die Umwandlung von Theca-abgeleiteten Androgenen (unter LH-Stimulation gebildet) in Östrogene ermöglichen — das Zwei-Zell-Zwei-Gonadotropin-Modell der follikulären Östrogensynthese — und induzieren mit Annäherung an den Eisprung zusätzlich die Expression von LH-Rezeptoren. Call-Exner-Körperchen, eosinophile Ansammlungen basallaminaähnlicher extrazellulärer Matrix, umgeben von Granulosazell-Rosetten, gelten als charakteristisches histologisches Merkmal der Granulosazell-Differenzierung. Nach dem LH-Anstieg und dem Eisprung durchlaufen murale Granulosazellen die Luteinisierung — Hypertrophie, Verlust der Proliferationsfähigkeit und eine steroidogene Verschiebung hin zu Progesteron — und werden zu Granulosa-Luteinzellen, dem Haupt-Zelltyp des Gelbkörpers (Corpus luteum), der das sekretorische Endometrium in der Lutealphase bis zur Einnistung aufrechterhält oder andernfalls in die Luteolyse übergeht.
