## 1. Scientific reference (the yardstick for verification)

The beige adipocyte (also called "brite", brown-in-white) is a thermogenic fat cell that arises **within white adipose tissue (WAT) depots** — chiefly subcutaneous fat (inguinal in rodents, subcutaneous abdominal/gluteal in humans) — rather than in the dedicated brown-fat depots. Unlike classical brown adipocytes, which come from a myogenic (Myf5+) precursor lineage and are constitutively thermogenic, beige adipocytes arise from a distinct progenitor pool related to white-fat precursors (PDGFRα+ stromovascular progenitors) either by **de novo differentiation** or by **transdifferentiation of existing white adipocytes**, and their thermogenic programme is **inducible, not constitutive**: at rest a beige cell can look almost like a white adipocyte (a large lipid droplet, low UCP1, sparse mitochondria), but on stimulation it remodels into a **paucilocular/multilocular** cell with several-to-many smaller lipid droplets and a markedly increased mitochondrial density — an intermediate morphology between resting white fat and constitutively brown fat, not a fixed third look. The two physiological triggers highlighted here are **cold exposure**, which drives sympathetic nerves to release **noradrenaline** onto **β3-adrenergic receptors** on the adipocyte surface, and **exercise**, during which skeletal muscle cleaves and releases the myokine **irisin** from its membrane precursor FNDC5; irisin acts on adipocytes (proposed via αV/β5 integrin) to induce the beiging/browning gene programme. Both pathways converge on **PGC-1α**-driven induction of **UCP1 (uncoupling protein 1)** in the inner mitochondrial membrane, which uncouples the mitochondrial proton gradient from ATP synthase so the energy is released as heat (non-shivering thermogenesis) — the same molecular mechanism as brown fat, just switched on rather than always-on. Beige adipocytes also carry the standard eukaryotic housekeeping machinery (rough ER, Golgi) and sit, like all adipose tissue, in a bed of capillaries and sympathetic nerve fibres. Because activating and expanding beige fat increases whole-body energy expenditure, it is an actively studied therapeutic target for obesity and metabolic disease ("browning agents").

Sources: [Wikipedia — Beige fat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beige_fat), [Wikipedia — Adipose tissue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adipose_tissue), [Wikipedia — Irisin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irisin), [Wu et al. 2012, Beige adipocytes are a distinct type of thermogenic fat cell in mouse and human, Cell (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22796012/), [Boström et al. 2012, A PGC1-α-dependent myokine that drives brown-fat-like development of white fat and thermogenesis, Nature (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22258508/), [Ikeda, Maretich & Kajimura 2018, The Common and Distinct Features of Brown and Beige Adipocytes, Trends in Endocrinology & Metabolism (PMC)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5985258/).

### Do NOT draw (scientifically misleading)
- **No single giant unilocular lipid droplet filling the whole cell with the nucleus squashed flat at the rim** — that is a resting *white* adipocyte; beige fat (especially once activated, which is the state we illustrate) is **paucilocular/multilocular** with several-to-many visible droplets.
- **No wall-to-wall dense packing of huge mitochondria dominating the entire cytoplasm like constitutive brown fat** — beige mitochondria are numerous and inducible but should read as clearly **intermediate**: fewer/smaller-in-effect than a classic brown adipocyte, far more than resting white fat.
- **No cell wall, nucleoid, plasmids or bacterial flagella** — this is a human animal cell, not a prokaryote.
- **No chloroplasts or large central plant-style vacuole.**
- **No cilia/flagella for locomotion** — adipocytes are stationary tissue cells.
- **Capillaries and nerve fibres, if shown, stay OUTSIDE the cell body** in the surrounding tissue context, never inside the cytoplasm.
- A single specimen (or a small readable cluster for tissue-context styles) — not a dense, unreadable confluent sheet where individual droplets/mitochondria can't be told apart.

**Scientific.**  
🇬🇧 The beige (or "brite", brown-in-white) adipocyte is a thermogenic cell that arises within white adipose tissue depots, chiefly subcutaneous fat, from a PDGFRα+ progenitor lineage distinct from the myogenic (Myf5+) precursors of classical brown fat, either via de novo differentiation or transdifferentiation of existing white adipocytes. Its thermogenic programme is inducible rather than constitutive: at baseline it can resemble a white adipocyte, but cold-induced sympathetic noradrenaline acting on β3-adrenergic receptors, or the exercise-induced myokine irisin (cleaved from FNDC5, proposed to signal via αV/β5 integrin), converge on PGC-1α-driven induction of UCP1 in the inner mitochondrial membrane. This remodels the cell into a paucilocular/multilocular phenotype with increased mitochondrial density, dissipating the proton-motive force as heat via UCP1-mediated proton leak (non-shivering thermogenesis) rather than driving ATP synthase. Because expanding functional beige fat mass increases whole-body energy expenditure, the beige adipocyte and its recruitment pathways (β3-adrenergic agonism, irisin/FNDC5 signalling) are a major focus of pharmacological research into obesity and metabolic disease.  
🇩🇪 Der beige (oder „Brite-“, brown-in-white) Adipozyt ist eine thermogene Zelle, die innerhalb von weißem Fettgewebe, vor allem subkutanem Fett, aus einer PDGFRα+-Vorläuferlinie entsteht, die sich von der myogenen (Myf5+) Vorläuferlinie des klassischen braunen Fetts unterscheidet - entweder durch De-novo-Differenzierung oder durch Transdifferenzierung bereits bestehender weißer Adipozyten. Ihr thermogenes Programm ist induzierbar, nicht konstitutiv: im Ruhezustand kann sie einem weißen Adipozyten ähneln, doch kälteinduziertes sympathisches Noradrenalin über β3-Adrenorezeptoren oder das belastungsinduzierte Myokin Irisin (abgespalten von FNDC5, vermutlich über das Integrin αV/β5 wirkend) münden beide in eine PGC-1α-vermittelte Induktion von UCP1 in der inneren Mitochondrienmembran. Dies formt die Zelle zu einem parvilokulären/multilokulären Phänotyp mit erhöhter Mitochondriendichte um, wodurch die protonenmotorische Kraft über den UCP1-vermittelten Protonenleckweg als Wärme abgegeben wird (zitterfreie Thermogenese), statt die ATP-Synthase anzutreiben. Da eine Vergrößerung der funktionellen beigen Fettmasse den Energieverbrauch des gesamten Körpers erhöht, sind der beige Adipozyt und seine Rekrutierungswege (β3-adrenerge Agonisten, Irisin/FNDC5-Signalgebung) ein zentraler Forschungsschwerpunkt der pharmakologischen Adipositas- und Stoffwechselforschung.
