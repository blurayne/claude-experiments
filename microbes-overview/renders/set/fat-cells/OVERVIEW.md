## Adipogenic progenitor (`adipogenic-progenitor`)
Mesenchymal-stem-cell-derived precursor that sits as a mural (pericyte-like) cell wrapped around small blood vessels in adipose tissue; still uncommitted, but usually differentiates into a fat cell. Compact, stellate body with several thin radiating processes, a large euchromatic nucleus, moderate rough ER/Golgi/mitochondria, and (once adipogenic signalling starts) a handful of small early lipid droplets.

## Beige adipocyte (`beige-adipocyte`)
In-between form: lives in white fat depots but can be switched on by cold or exercise to a heat-producing state ('browning'). Responds to irisin (from muscle) and noradrenaline. A therapeutic target against obesity.

## Brown adipocyte (`brown-adipocyte`)
Body heater: many small lipid droplets and unusually many mitochondria. Burns fat directly into heat (via UCP1) rather than ATP.

## Lipoblast (`lipoblast`)
Early precursor from a mesenchymal stem cell. Picks up the first small lipid droplets before maturing into a preadipocyte. Key in embryonic fat development; reappear in liposarcomas.

## White adipocyte (`white-adipocyte`)
Classic fat cell: a single huge lipid droplet pushes the nucleus and cytoplasm to the rim. Stores energy for lean times; releases leptin to signal the brain about fat reserves.
