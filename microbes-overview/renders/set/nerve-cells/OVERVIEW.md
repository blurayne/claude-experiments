## Astrocyte (`astrocyte`)
Star-shaped glial support cell with many radiating processes ending in perivascular end-feet on brain capillaries; feeds and buffers neurons, recycles glutamate, and helps form the blood-brain barrier.

## Microglia (`microglia`)
Brain-resident macrophage with a small soma and many thin, highly branched processes that constantly probe the tissue, clear debris and prune synapses; yolk-sac derived and the CNS's first line of immune defence.

## Motor neuron (Motoneuron) (`motor-neuron`)
Large multipolar nerve cell of the brainstem/spinal-cord anterior horn, with a star-shaped soma full of Nissl bodies, many branching dendrites and a single long myelinated axon that can reach a meter in length, ending at the motor end plate on a skeletal-muscle fibre.

## Neuron (`neuron`)
Compute-and-signal cell of the nervous system: a star-shaped soma with branching dendrites collects synaptic input, sums it up and, if threshold is reached, fires an electrical impulse down its single axon to the next cell.

## Oligodendrocyte (`oligodendrocyte`)
CNS glial cell with a small, dense-nucleus cell body and several non-branching processes, each ending in a flattened membrane sheet that spirals into compact myelin around a segment (internode) of a nearby axon; one oligodendrocyte myelinates several axons at once. Partly fuels the axons it wraps via a lactate shuttle; its myelin is the target in multiple sclerosis.

## Schwann cell (`schwann-cell`)
Myelin-making glial cell of the peripheral nervous system: a single Schwann cell spirally wraps its own plasma membrane around one segment (internode) of one axon, with its flattened nucleus pressed into the outer myelin it built. Also drives peripheral nerve regeneration after injury by clearing debris and guiding regrowing axons.
