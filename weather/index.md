# weather

Notes on getting real weather data out of two very different sources: Google's WeatherNext 3 AI ensemble and the German Weather Service's open data server.

- [**API.md**](API.md) — what each source requires (accounts, approvals, keys, formats, licences), the concrete endpoints and dataset identifiers, and which door to pick for which kind of project. Endpoints were probed live on 7 September 2026; the doc ends with a log of exactly what was checked and what could not be.
